Hi, I'm Martha.

My way into design wasn't a straight line. I started in marketing: a degree in Ukraine, then a job as a marketing specialist. Somewhere along the way I realised I was more curious about how things work for people than how they're sold, so I switched to UX/UI.

I learned the craft through Google's UX design courses, from the very first research and wireframes to testing ideas early and building high-fidelity prototypes in Figma. Then I went straight into practice. Both of my apps started with something from my own life. You'll find more about that in the case studies.

Outside of design, I'm very much into sport: I've been a CrossFit lover for almost three years now. In my free time I really enjoy reading. My favourite books are Arch of Triumph by Erich Maria Remarque, Stoner by John Williams and The Secret History by Donna Tartt. If you've read any of them, I'd love to hear what you thought.

Travel has also been a big part of my life for the past four years, and I've visited 15 countries so far. I like to explore, ask questions, get to know people and get better at what I love.
